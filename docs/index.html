Now Playing look

Shown in the large now playing view while a track is playing. 0 keeps the background still. The tempo is looked up on Deezer. Not every song has one; those simply don’t pulse. Shrinks and elevates the clock into the upper corner when music is actively playing on the Now Playing screen, giving the artwork, text, and lyrics more room.

Weather provider

Weather screens

Runs the sky and sun from midnight to the current hour upon entering Weather. Opens the Sun view at golden hour, sunrise, sunset and solar noon, the Moon view at moonrise and full moon, and the Weather overview during rain or storms. 10 minutes after the event is over it goes back to Home, unless you picked another screen in the meantime. Warnings are worked out from the forecast (strong gusts, heavy rain or snow, storms, freezing rain, heat, severe cold, fog). They are not official HungaroMet warnings.

Setup

Walks through language, location, photos, music and look again. Your current choices are kept as the starting point.

Android app

The app starts by itself after the tablet boots once Android allows it to “display over other apps”. The frame itself updates automatically; only changes to the app shell need a new download.
