    Performance

    Light mode turns off frosted-glass blur, lowers animation frame rates and uses smaller images.

    Screen brightness

    The screen fades gradually over 45 minutes. Drag the slider to preview it.

    Tap the screen to brighten it for 5 seconds. The darker the screen, the smaller the step, so it doesn't dazzle you at night.

    Night screen

    During these hours the frame doesn't jump to sky events. Tap the screen to see the normal screen for a minute; alarms still ring and music still shows. In the morning it goes back to Home.

    Now Playing look

    Shown in the large now playing view while a track is playing. 0 keeps the background still. The colours swirl with the music and get a push on every beat. Tempo uses the song's speed from Deezer, so it can be a little ahead of or behind the real beat. Spotify also uses where you are in the song (only when the music source is Spotify). Microphone hits the beat exactly when the music plays in the same room; the sound is analysed on this device and never recorded or sent anywhere (Android app 2.2.0 or newer, or a browser). If it can't hear a beat, it falls back to the tempo.

    When an album has an animated cover on Apple Music, it plays in place of the picture. Android app only (Apple's website can't be read by web pages); uses a few MB of data per album. Shrinks and elevates the clock into the upper corner when music is actively playing on the Now Playing screen, giving the artwork, text, and lyrics more room.

    Weather provider

    Weather screens

    Runs the sky and sun from midnight to the current hour upon entering Weather. Opens the Sun view at golden hour, sunrise, sunset and solar noon, the Moon view at moonrise and full moon, and the Weather overview during rain or storms. 5 minutes after the event is over it goes back to Home, unless you picked another screen in the meantime. Official warnings are shown where they are available: most of Europe through MeteoAlarm (in the Android app) and the US through the National Weather Service. Everywhere else, and in between, warnings are worked out from the forecast (strong gusts, heavy rain or snow, storms, freezing rain, heat, severe cold, fog).

    Debugging

    Shows a panel with live details such as the song’s BPM, frame rate, memory, weather data, photo info and timers. Useful when something does not work as expected.

    Setup

    Walks through language, location, photos, music and look again. Your current choices are kept as the starting point.

    Android app

    The app starts by itself after the tablet boots once Android allows it to “display over other apps”. The frame itself updates automatically; only changes to the app shell need a new download.

    Lock

    Asks for a 4-digit PIN before Settings or setup open, so children or guests can't change things or see your keys. It's a simple lock, not real security: clearing the app's or browser's data removes it, together with all settings.

    Backup

    Photos aren't included (choose them again on the new device), and Spotify has to be connected again. Don't share a backup that has keys in it.

    Updates

    Report a problem

    Opens a ready-to-send bug report on GitHub with the frame version, device and recent errors. API keys, logins and your location are left out, and you can read everything before sending.
